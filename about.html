My Works About Me

I'm Ari

Since I was young, I've known I was meant to be an artist. I had a deep interest in science and history, but there's nothing as creatively liberating and self-expressive as art—and once they put math in science class, I lost all interest!

However, to keep some of that touch of science, I like to say that art breaks the 'law of conservation of mass.' We artists build ideas out of nothing but our minds, and eventually, they find their way into the physical world for a multitude of people to experience and enjoy. The best part is knowing I have contributed my skills to a project that made someone's day. Creating for the sake of something bigger than myself has always been my dream.

If you'd really like to learn more, feel free to message me on LinkedIn or Instagram.
